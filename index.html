A NOTE ON PRIVACY

Your résumé, job description, recordings, and reflections stay in this browser. The app does not send them to an AI service or a server.

  • Question matching uses straightforward keyword rules.
  • Your score comes from the ratings you select against the rubric.
  • Video is recorded or attached for your review. It is not automatically assessed.
  • This practice score is a coaching aid, not a hiring decision or immigration advice.
